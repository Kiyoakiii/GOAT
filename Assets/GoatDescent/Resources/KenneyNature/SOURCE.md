# Kenney Nature Kit

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit

Author: Kenney (https://kenney.nl/)

License: CC0. Original license is preserved in License.txt.

Imported subset: 13 original OBJ models with their MTL material definitions.
Four detailed tall pines, one small pine, four large rocks, three tall rocks,
and one flat stone. Runtime materials are recolored for the alpine setting.

Placement and recoloring: Scripts/Playground/MountainAssetScenery.cs.
Models are instantiated when MovementTestScene enters Play.
